What the Paper Can and Cannot Claim

The archive supports a claim about reported calibrations; only #125 can support a claim about the device.

Can claim

  • On ibm_fez, calibrations are a level plus scatter; the rolling band is the bar; stale per qubit beats fresh uniform (NC-081, NC-083 to NC-085)
  • After #125: the first pre-registered hardware comparison of reading, level and band as design-time predictors
  • The fuzzy calibration-to-channel map: a novel combination of textbook parts

Cannot claim

  • Device physics from the archive: it shows persistence of reported T1 estimates
  • Outcome-space resolution on hardware: unmeasured until #125’s rows exist
  • Drift bracketing as first pitched: no fuzzy band has been scored against the rolling band yet
  • Anything beyond ibm_fez: no second backend is measured

If S1 passes: quantum venue (QST, npj QI, PR Applied)

Regardless: fuzzy-systems venue (IEEE TFS, Fuzzy Sets and Systems, Applied Soft Computing), #125 as its hardware section
